This repository does **not** redistribute the survey dataset. Obtain it from the original authors' Zenodo record:

**Pertiwi & Adiningrum (2026).** *When Culture Meets AI: A Cross-Cultural Study of Generative AI Dependency Among U.S. and Indonesian Business Students*. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21013786

After downloading the workbook, save it in this directory with the exact name:

`When Culture Meets AI - Survey Dataset.xlsx`

The notebook reads the `Survey Data` and `Codebook` sheets. Respect the dataset's license and terms.
